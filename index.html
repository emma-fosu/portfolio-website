Areas of Expertise

  • Statistical Analysis

    Apply descriptive and Inferential Statistics, Regression, A/B Testing and Hypothesis Testing to analyze data, identify trends and relationships, validate assumptions, and support evidence-based decision-making.

  • ETL Pipelining and Data Modeling

    Design and maintain ETL workflows to extract, clean, and transform large, raw data from multiple sources and build scalable data models to support efficient querying, reporting, and analytics.

    Transform complex data into clear, engaging visuals through a logical narrative. Design custom charts using Deneb (Vega-Lite) and Vega to highlight patterns and comparisons, ensuring insights are understandable, accurate, and tailored to both technical and non-technical viewers.

    Build scripts and scheduled workflows with Python, SQL, and Power Automate to streamline data extraction, transformation, and reporting, reducing manual effort, minimizing errors, and improving efficiency across analytics pipelines and business processes.

  • Business and Domain Acumen

    Translate business questions into analytical problems by understanding operations, finance, and customer behavior. Identify relevant metrics, interpret data in context, and align analysis with organizational goals to support informed decision-making

  • Predictive Analytics

    Leverages historical and current data to build predictive models for outcomes like revenue forecasting, customer lifetime value, customer churn prevention, marketing mix, and fraud detection.

  • Data Quality, Governance, and Ethics

    Maintain accurate, consistent, and reliable data through validation and cleaning. Implement governance, access controls, and ethical handling of sensitive information to ensure trustworthy, compliant, and high-quality analytics practices.
