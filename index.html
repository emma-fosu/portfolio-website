Areas of Expertise

  • Statistical Analysis

    Apply descriptive and Inferential Statistics, Regression, A/B Testing and Hypothesis Testing to analyze data, identify trends and relationships and validate assumptions.

  • ETL Pipelining and Data Modeling

    Design and maintain ETL workflows to extract, clean, and transform large, raw data from multiple sources and build scalable data models to support efficient querying, reporting, and analytics.

  • Data Visualization and Storytelling

    Transform complex data into clear, engaging visuals through a logical narrative. Design custom charts using Deneb (Vega-Lite) and Vega to effectively communicate to both technical and non-technical audiences.

  • Automation

    Build scripts and scheduled workflows with Python, SQL, Power Automate and Google App Script to streamline data extraction, transformation, and reporting faster, reducing manual effort and minimizing errors.

  • Business and Domain Acumen

    Translate business questions into analytical problems by understanding operations, finance, products and customer behavior of the organization and identify relevant metrics that suit organizational goals.

  • Predictive Analytics

    Leverages historical and current data to build predictive models for outcomes like revenue forecasting, customer lifetime value, customer churn prevention, marketing mix, and fraud detection.

  • Data Quality, Governance, and Ethics

    Maintain accurate, consistent, and reliable data through validation and cleaning. Implement governance, access controls, and ethical handling of sensitive information to ensure trustworthy, compliant, and high-quality analytics practices.
